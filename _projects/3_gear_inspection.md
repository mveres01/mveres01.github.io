### Overview

To deploy automated gear inspection systems directly in-situ within automotive manufacturing lines, systems must operate rapidly within assembly cycle times while reliably distinguishing multiple defect types across complex helical tooth surfaces.

### System Features

- **Rapid 7.5s Inspection:** Complete 360-degree tooth geometry acquisition and defect classification executed within strict manufacturing line limits.
- **Multi-Defect Classification:** Simultaneously classifies three distinct industrial defect categories using deep convolutional architectures.
- **Production Integration:** Designed for robust optical operation on unpainted machined metal surfaces subject to reflection and surface variations.
