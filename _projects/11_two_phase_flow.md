### Overview

Real-time monitoring of two-phase gas-liquid slug flow in metallic pipelines is critical across industrial processes, chemical manufacturing, and offshore infrastructure. Traditional measurement techniques often require radioactive densitometry or invasive instrumentation that disrupts flow dynamics.

### Key Highlights

- **Non-Intrusive Sensing:** Coupled a non-intrusive, wall-integrated multi-capacitance sensor with advanced signal processing to acquire spatial-temporal permittivity profiles.
- **Deep Learning Inversion:** Deployed neural network models to accurately reconstruct two-phase slug flow regimes and hydrodynamic velocity fields in real time.
- **Experimental Validation:** Verified on multi-phase pipeline flow loops in collaboration with Dr. Shahriyar Ghazanfari Holagh, Dr. Wael Ahmed, and Dr. Medhat Moussa.
