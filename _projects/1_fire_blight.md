### Overview

Fire blight is a highly infectious and destructive bacterial disease affecting apple and pear orchards. While managing the disease is critical to orchard health, identifying symptoms early is an exceptionally difficult challenge that typically demands trained expert scouts.

### Key Contributions

- **Multi-Channel Sensing:** Leverages paired visible (RGB) and near-infrared (NIR) imaging channels to highlight spectral reflectance differences caused by bacterial infection.
- **Density Estimation Learning:** Employs density map estimation to detect and localize individual symptom clusters even under variable natural illumination and dense canopy occlusion.
- **Field Evaluation:** Evaluated on real-world orchard datasets to demonstrate practical detection accuracy before extensive necrotic damage spreads.
