### Overview

In commercial floriculture, detecting flower buds on potted chrysanthemum plants is vital for automated disbudding and grading before market distribution.

### Highlights

- **Radial Hough Transform:** Designed a tailored feature extraction and voting procedure robust to dense green leaves and overlapping foliage.
- **Physical Robot Validation:** Validated localization precision using an eye-in-hand industrial robot arm for automated manipulation.
